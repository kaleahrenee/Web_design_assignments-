Why Training Is Important

Training is an important part of having a well-behaved dog. It helps dogs understand what their owners expect from them. Teaching basic commands can make everyday activities easier and safer. Dogs can learn commands such as sit, stay, come, and down. Training also gives dogs a chance to use their brains and learn new things. Spending time training your dog can create a stronger bond between you and your pet.

Understanding Dog Behavior

Dogs communicate with people through their actions and body language. A wagging tail can sometimes show excitement, while lowered ears may mean a dog feels nervous. Dogs may bark to get attention or communicate that something is happening. Positive reinforcement is a helpful way to teach good behavior. Giving treats, praise, and encouragement can motivate dogs to learn. Being patient and consistent helps dogs feel comfortable during training.

Some basic commands every dog should learn include:

  • Sit
  • Stay
  • Come
  • Leave it
  • Down

Learn more about training dogs at American Kennel Club Training and ASPCA Dog Care.

Training Tips!

Always reward your dog when it does something correctly. Treats and praise can make learning fun and exciting.

Keep training sessions short so your dog does not become bored or frustrated. Remember that every dog learns at its own pace!
